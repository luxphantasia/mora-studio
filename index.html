Arrange

Tracks

Your whole song at a glance: a row for each singer and each audio track, laid out bar by bar.

  • Tap an empty row to add a part, drag to move it, pull either end to resize
  • Trim a part from either side without losing what's inside
  • Hold the ruler to loop a bar, drag across to loop more
  • Room before bar 1 for a pickup or a count-in
  • Snap from whole bars down to 1/32 and triplets, or let it follow your zoom
  • Pinch to zoom each way, flick to scroll, and pull past the edges with a gentle bounce

Arrange

Markers

Change the time signature or key mid-song, or name your sections, right on the ruler.

  • One marker can change the time signature, the key, or just name a section
  • The current signature and key always show at the left edge
  • Drag a marker to move it
Next: Automation →

Arrange

Automation

Shape tempo, volume, pan and any effect setting over the course of the song.

  • Pick what to automate from one menu
  • Tap to add a point, drag to move it, and let points glide into the next
  • Clear or hide a lane with one tap
Next: Piano roll →

Write

Piano roll

Write the melody with your fingers, with each note's syllable printed right on it.

  • Tap to place a note, drag to move it, pull its end to change its length
  • Every note sings as you tap or drag it, so you hear the melody as you write
  • Hold for a selection box; copy, cut and paste whole phrases
  • Mark は, へ or を to be sung as spoken (わ, え, お)
  • Play the keyboard to try out a pitch
Next: Singing →

Sing

Singing

Pick a voice and your melody sings, right on your phone.

  • Add your own voicebanks and choose one per singer
  • Change a note and only that phrase is redone, so you're never kept waiting
  • Long vowels (ー) and particles are sung naturally
  • Hear a guide tone wherever the voice isn't ready yet
Next: Lyrics →

Write

Lyrics

Write the words like in a notes app, and see at a glance where they fit the melody.

  • Readings for kanji filled in for you, and easy to change
  • Particles are spotted as you write: 私は sings わ, 空へ sings え
  • Type in romaji or kana with suggestions that fit the phrase
  • Every line shows whether it fits its phrase
  • Write the words first and place them later, or fill in the melody's phrases
Next: Mixer →

Mix

Mixer

A full mixer in your pocket: a channel for every singer and track, buses, groups and the master.

  • Hold a channel's name to move it; drag effects between slots and channels
  • As many effects per channel as you like
  • Solo tracks or whole buses
  • Group channels to move their volume, pan, mute and solo together
  • Save snapshots of the whole mix, and undo any change
  • Studio-grade sound that stays light on your battery
Next: Effects →

Mix

Effects

Eight built-in effects, each with presets, A/B compare and undo.

  • EQ bands that react to the music
  • Factory presets for every effect, and save your own
  • Flip between two settings to compare, saved with the song
Next: Export →

Share

Export

Share the finished song, or take it into another app.

  • Pick the quality you want
  • Export the whole song or just the loop
  • Players and stores show your song's title and artist
  • Files keep your song's name, in Japanese too
Next: History and settings →

Everywhere

History and settings

Every change is saved as you go, and you can always step back.

  • Your songs are kept safe, even if you reinstall the app
  • Keep mixer changes out of your undo list if you like
  • Pause returns to where you started, or resumes, as you choose
